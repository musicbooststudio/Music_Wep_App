Documentation & How-To Guide

A complete walkthrough of every feature and how to use Music Boost.

🚀 Getting Started

  1. Launch the app — open the Music Boost interface in your browser.
  2. Upload stems — drag & drop audio files (WAV, MP3, and more) onto the upload zone, or click to browse for multiple files at once.
  3. Wait for analysis — the app automatically detects the key and tempo of your first stem.
  4. Press Play to preview your mix and start shaping your sound.

🎚️ Track Controls

  • Gain — adjust the volume of each stem from −24 dB to +12 dB.
  • Pan — position the stem anywhere between left (−1) and right (+1).
  • Mute — silence a stem without removing it from the mix.
  • Solo — isolate one stem; enabling solo automatically mutes all others.

🎛️ Effects & Tone Shaping

Open the Effects panel on each track to access:

  • Denoiser (0–100%) — a noise gate that reduces background hiss and room tone.
  • Low (−12 to +12 dB) — a low-shelf filter that boosts or cuts the bass region.
  • Saturation (0–100%) — adds analog-style warmth and harmonics.
  • Tube Drive (0–100%) — emulates classic tube distortion for grit and character.
  • Reverb (0–100%) — adds space and depth with a feedback delay.
  • HPF (20–500 Hz) — high-pass filter removes unwanted low rumble.
  • Low Mid / Mid / Presence — peaking EQ bands for surgical tonal control.
  • LPF (2000–20000 Hz) — low-pass filter tames harsh highs.

📈 Mastering Your Mix

The Mastering panel applies global processing to the whole mix:

  • Master Gain — overall output level (−24 to +12 dB).
  • Compression Threshold — the level at which compression kicks in.
  • Compression Ratio — how strongly the signal is compressed above the threshold.
  • Output Meter — a live level meter so you can avoid clipping.

💾 Exporting Your Mix

  1. Click the Export Mix button.
  2. If you aren't signed in, login with Google or use the Demo Account.
  3. Complete the one-time $2.99 payment via the secure Stripe checkout.
  4. Your fully processed mixdown downloads automatically as a WAV file.

⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts

  • Space — play or stop playback.
  • Ctrl / Cmd + E — open the export flow.

Tip: Shortcuts are disabled while typing in any input field so you can rename and tweak safely.
